How it works

What the Order Taking & Quotation Generation AI automation does

From the moment an order lands in your inbox to a confirmed quotation saved in your records — CutCost AI handles the repetitive steps, and your manager stays in control of approvals.

  1. Receive order

    Order is received through your existing systems (order system or emails).

  2. Extract order info

    CutCost AI works with your existing systems to extract order information.

  3. Check for missing info

    CutCost AI checks for missing order information.

  4. Obtain missing info

    If anything is missing, CutCost AI queries the customer via email.

  5. Generate quotation

    Once all info is in, CutCost AI checks your rate cards and generates a quotation.

  6. Approve quotation

    Your human manager approves the quotation.

    You stay in control

  7. Send quotation

    CutCost AI sends the quotation to the customer for confirmation.

  8. Capture confirmation

    CutCost AI captures the customer's confirmation of the quotation.

  9. Save data

    The confirmation is saved in a secure platform maintained by you.
