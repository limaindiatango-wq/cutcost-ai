Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to register?

Yes — creating a free account (email + password) lets you manage your own booking and cancel it later from the My Registration page.

What happens if a session is full?

You're automatically placed on that session's waitlist instead of being blocked. An organizer can move you to Registered from the dashboard if a seat frees up.

Can I change my session after registering?

Cancel your current booking from the My Registration page, then register again for a different session.

Is my data saved anywhere?

Yes — registrations are saved to a live Supabase database tied to your account.
